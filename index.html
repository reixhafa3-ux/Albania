06 · Business manners & etiquette — in Albanian: mirësjellja

Relationships first, business second

Greeting

Firm handshake and eye contact; close colleagues may kiss on both cheeks.

Titles

Use “Zoti” (Mr) or “Zonja” (Mrs) + surname until invited to use first names.

Coffee culture

Deals often start over a coffee — never refuse the invitation.

“Besa”

A given word of honour is sacred: keep your promises.

Head gestures

Traditionally a nod can mean “no” and a shake “yes” — confirm out loud.

Time & dress

Be on time even if meetings start late; dress formally at first.

08 · My verdict

Would I live and work in Albania?

Pros

  • Very cheap public transport (€17/month)
  • Wages growing fast (+25% minimum wage)
  • Booming tourism, IT and energy jobs
  • Mountains, two seas, Mediterranean lifestyle

Cons

  • Salaries about 4× lower than in France
  • Rent in Tirana ≈ one average net salary
  • Fuel among the most expensive in Europe
  • Youth unemployment near 18%

Yes — if I find a skilled job (engineering, IT, tourism) paid above the average salary.
